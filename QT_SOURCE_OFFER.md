# Qt / PySide6 source offer for OculiDoC v0.1.1

The official OculiDoC v0.1.1 Windows packages dynamically link unmodified
PySide6 and Qt 6.11 components distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License version 3 (LGPL-3.0), as an alternative to their other available
licenses.

The corresponding LGPL-licensed library source and the information needed to
rebuild or relink those libraries will be provided on request, at no charge
other than the reasonable cost of physical transfer, for at least three years
after the last public distribution of OculiDoC v0.1.1. Request it through:

https://github.com/Etymodes/OculiDoC/issues

The unmodified upstream source is also published by The Qt Company at:

- https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
- https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
- https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/

The OculiDoC v0.1.1 application source and Windows build configuration are in
the public `v0.1.1` tag of:

https://github.com/Etymodes/OculiDoC

The Qt/PySide shared libraries remain separate files in the installed
`_internal/PySide6` directory. OculiDoC does not prohibit replacing those
libraries with a compatible user-built version or reverse engineering solely
for debugging such modifications, as permitted by the LGPL.
